Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Who we are

Qintar Ventures FZ LLC ("Qintar", "we", "us") is a business transformation advisory registered with RAKEZ in Ras Al Khaimah, UAE. This policy explains how we handle personal data collected through www.qintarventures.com. You can reach us at info@qintarventures.com.

What we collect

  • Details you give us through the call back form: your name, phone number, email address and message, plus the page you submitted the form from.
  • Details you give us when you email or call us.
  • Details you give when you book a call through our booking page, such as your name, email address and any answers you provide.
  • Technical data that our hosting provider and font provider receive automatically when you load the site, such as your IP address and browser type.

We do not run advertising or tracking cookies. When you submit the form, your browser stores a timestamp on your device to prevent accidental duplicate submissions.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your call back request and discuss your needs.
  • To scope and provide advisory services if you become a client.
  • To keep reasonable business records.
  • To protect the site from spam and misuse.

We rely on your consent, given when you submit the form, and on our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We use service providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase, which hosts the database that stores your request.
  • Resend, which delivers the email alert that tells us about your request.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.
  • Google Calendar, which runs the booking page if you choose to book a call, and Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces used on the site.

We may also disclose personal data where the law requires it.

International transfers

Our service providers may process data outside the UAE. We choose providers that apply appropriate safeguards to personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep your request for as long as needed to respond to you and to maintain reasonable business records. After that we delete it or remove the details that identify you.

Security

The site uses HTTPS. Requests are stored in a database that visitors cannot read, and access is limited to people at Qintar who need it. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data, you may ask us to give you access to your personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, or withdraw your consent. Email us at info@qintarventures.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
