HOW WE OPERATE

One Umbrella, Seven Departments

Business Transformation is the umbrella under which Qintar operates. The Principal and Managing Partner owns every client relationship and engagement design, and is personally involved across all departments as integrator and quality owner. Each department is led by a senior specialist; Qintar deploys the right operator for the need, with coordination of delivery and accountability to the client throughout.

Qintar's senior team works on a fractional basis, prioritizing client engagements alongside other primary commitments. This is a deliberate operating model: it is how clients get senior-level judgment without paying for senior-level idle time. Scoping and timelines are set accordingly, with realistic lead times built in rather than promised and missed.

HOW WE WORK TOGETHER

Engagement Models

Retainer

Ongoing senior access on a fixed monthly scope. Suited to functions that need continuous oversight: fractional CFO leadership, compliance oversight, or advisory continuity.

Project-Based

A defined scope with clear phases, milestones and deliverables. Suited to discrete initiatives: due diligence, an ERP implementation, a GTM launch, or a restructuring.

Interim Cover

Senior leadership placed into a gap for a defined period, covering a vacancy, a transition, or a period of accelerated growth the current team cannot absorb alone.
