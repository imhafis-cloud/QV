How we work together

Engagement models

Every engagement is a defined service or a governed project with defined phases, matched to what the client needs.

Retainer

Ongoing senior access on a fixed monthly scope. Suited to functions that need continuous oversight: fractional CFO leadership, compliance oversight or advisory continuity.

Project-based

A defined scope with clear phases, milestones and deliverables. Suited to discrete initiatives: due diligence, an ERP implementation, a GTM launch or a restructuring.

Interim cover

Senior leadership placed into a gap for a defined period, covering a vacancy, a transition or a period of accelerated growth the current team cannot absorb alone.

Qintar's senior team works on a fractional basis, alongside other primary commitments. This is a deliberate operating model: clients get senior-level judgment without paying for senior-level idle time. Scoping and timelines are agreed in writing, with realistic lead times built in.
